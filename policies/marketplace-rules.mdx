---
title: "Marketplace Rules"
description: "The rules that apply to everyone on Trevo."
---

These rules apply to everyone who uses Trevo. They exist to keep the marketplace safe, fair, and reliable.

## For all users

- Be honest and respectful in all interactions.
- Provide accurate information in your account, listings, and communications.
- Keep all transactions on Trevo.
- Do not attempt to circumvent Trevo's fees, systems, or security measures.
- Do not use Trevo for any unlawful purpose.
- Do not create multiple accounts to evade restrictions.

## Orders and payments

- Do not request or offer payment outside of Trevo.
- Do not use stolen, fraudulent, or unauthorized payment methods.

## Enforcement

Trevo may remove listings, restrict features, or suspend accounts that violate these rules. Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent removal from the marketplace.

See also [Seller Rules](/policies/seller-rules) and [Prohibited listings and activities](/policies/prohibited-listings).
