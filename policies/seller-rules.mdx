---
title: "Seller Rules"
description: "What Trevo expects from every seller."
---

Sellers are the foundation of Trevo. These rules set out what we expect from everyone who sells on the marketplace, in addition to the [Marketplace Rules](/policies/marketplace-rules).

## Listings

- List only products you own or are authorized to sell, and that you are able to deliver.
- Describe your products accurately and completely. Do not mislead buyers.
- Keep your price, stock, and delivery time up to date.
- Use the correct category and product for each listing.

## Delivery

- Deliver orders within the delivery time shown on your listing.
- Deliver exactly what was described.
- Follow the delivery method stated in your listing.

## Conduct

- Communicate professionally and respectfully with buyers.
- Do not ask buyers to pay, communicate, or complete orders outside of Trevo.
- Do not manipulate reviews or ratings.
- Do not share buyers' personal information.

## Account access

- Do not attempt to retrieve, recover, or reuse accounts or items after they have been sold.
- Protect your own account and do not share access with others.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal of listings, restrictions on your seller account, or permanent suspension.
