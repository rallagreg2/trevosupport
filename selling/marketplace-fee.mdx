---
title: "Trevo marketplace fee"
description: "How Trevo's 15% marketplace fee works."
---

Trevo charges sellers a **15% marketplace fee** on completed sales. The fee helps cover payment processing, platform security, and the ongoing operation of the marketplace.

## How the fee is calculated

The fee is 15% of the sale price of an order.

| Order total | Marketplace fee (15%) |
| --- | --- |
| 10.00 | 1.50 |
| 50.00 | 7.50 |
| 100.00 | 15.00 |

## When the fee applies

The fee applies to completed sales.

## Pricing tip

When setting your price, take the fee into account so you know what you will receive from each sale.
