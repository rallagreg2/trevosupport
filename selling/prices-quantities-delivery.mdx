---
title: "Prices, quantities, and delivery times"
description: "How to set prices, stock, minimum orders, and delivery times on a listing."
---

These settings determine what buyers see on your listing and how your offer ranks against others.

## Price

Set a competitive price per unit or a total price. Buyers can filter and compare offers by price, so check what similar listings are charging.

## Quantity and stock

- **Stock** is the number of units you currently have available. Keep it accurate so buyers can only order what you can deliver.
- **Minimum quantity** is the smallest amount a buyer can order in a single purchase.

## Delivery time

The delivery time is your estimate of how long you need to complete an order. Buyers can filter listings by delivery time, so a realistic and fast estimate helps your offer stand out.

<Warning>
Deliver within the time you state. Consistently late deliveries can affect your seller rating and standing.
</Warning>
