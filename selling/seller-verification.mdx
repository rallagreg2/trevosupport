---

Seller verification confirms the identity of sellers on Trevo and helps protect buyers and sellers from fraud.

## Why we verify sellers

- It builds trust between buyers and sellers.
- It helps us prevent fraud and abuse on the marketplace.
- It keeps our community safe.

## How to get verified

Open the **Seller Verification** page from the footer of Trevo and follow the on-screen instructions. You may be asked to provide identification details. This information is handled in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](/policies/legal-policies).

## Verification status

Your verification status is shown in your account. If more information is needed, you will be notified by email.

<Note>
Only provide verification details through Trevo's official pages. Trevo staff will never ask for your password.
</Note>
