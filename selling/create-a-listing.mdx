---
title: "How to create a listing"
description: "Publish and manage offers on the Trevo marketplace."
---

A clear, accurate listing helps buyers trust your offer and complete their purchase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a category and product">
    Select the category (for example Currency, Accounts, or Items) and the game or product you want to sell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a title and description">
    Use a descriptive title that states exactly what you are selling and the delivery method. In the description, include anything a buyer should know before purchasing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your price and stock">
    Enter your price per unit or total price, your available stock, and the minimum order quantity. See [Prices, quantities, and delivery times](/selling/prices-quantities-delivery).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a delivery method and time">
    Choose how you will deliver the order and set a realistic delivery estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add delivery instructions">
    Tell the buyer what they need to do to receive their order, for example when you will contact them or what information you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Review your listing and publish it. It will appear on the product page for buyers to find.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing your listings

You can edit or remove your listings at any time from your seller dashboard. Keep your stock and prices up to date so buyers see accurate information.

<Tip>
Listings with a complete description and clear delivery instructions tend to attract more buyers.
</Tip>
