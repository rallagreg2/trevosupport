---
title: "Currency bundles and top-up denominations"
description: "How to offer per-unit currency and fixed top-up bundles."
---

Currency and top-up products can be sold either per unit or in fixed bundles, depending on the category.

## Per-unit listings

In-game currency is typically sold per unit. You set a price per unit, a minimum order quantity, and your available stock. Buyers choose any quantity within those limits.

## Bundles and denominations

Top-up products are typically sold in fixed denominations, for example a set amount of in-game currency at a set price. When creating a listing, add the denominations you can deliver and a price for each.

## Tips

- Offer the denominations buyers search for most often.
- Keep your prices consistent across bundles so larger bundles offer fair value.
- Remove denominations you can no longer deliver.
