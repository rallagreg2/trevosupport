---
title: "How to become a seller"
description: "What you need to start selling on Trevo."
---

Anyone with a Trevo account can apply to sell on the marketplace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and verify your account">
    Register a Trevo account and confirm your email address. See [Creating an account](/account/create-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete seller verification">
    Seller verification helps keep the marketplace trustworthy for everyone. See [Seller verification](/selling/seller-verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Seller Rules">
    Review the [Seller Rules](/policies/seller-rules) and the [Marketplace Rules](/policies/marketplace-rules) so you know what is expected of sellers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first listing">
    Follow [How to create a listing](/selling/create-a-listing) to publish your first offer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you start

Only sell products that you are authorized to sell and are able to deliver. Listings that break our rules may be removed, and repeated violations can lead to account restrictions.
