---
title: "How to find a listing"
description: "Browse categories, search, and filter to find the right offer."
---

Trevo organizes listings into categories so you can quickly find what you're looking for, whether that is currency, accounts, items, top ups, boosting, or commissions.

## Browse by category

1. Open the category menu at the top of any Trevo page (for example **Currency**, **Accounts**, or **Items**).
2. Select the game or community you are interested in.
3. Choose the specific product, such as a currency type or item, to see all available listings.

## Search

Use the search icon in the top navigation bar to look up a game, product, or seller directly.

## Filter and compare offers

On a product page, use the available filters to narrow your results. Depending on the category, you can filter by device or platform, price, and delivery time.

Each listing card shows:

- The listing title
- The seller's name and rating
- The price per unit or total price
- The seller's estimated delivery time

Select a listing to open the full offer page, where you can review the description, delivery instructions, and seller details before you buy.

<Tip>
Before purchasing, check the seller's reviews and delivery estimate, and read the listing's delivery instructions carefully.
</Tip>
