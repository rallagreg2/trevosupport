---
title: "Accepted payment methods"
description: "The payment methods accepted on Trevo and how payments are handled."
---

Trevo accepts major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. The payment options available to you are shown at checkout.

## Payment security

Payments are processed by a secure third-party payment provider. Trevo does not store your full card details.

## Currency

Prices are shown in your selected currency. Your bank may apply its own conversion rate or fees if your card is issued in a different currency.

## Payment declined?

If your payment is declined, see [I can't complete checkout](/buying/checkout-issues).
