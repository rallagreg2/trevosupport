---
title: "How to place an order"
description: "A step-by-step guide to purchasing from a seller on Trevo."
---

Placing an order on Trevo takes only a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an offer">
    Browse or search for the product you want, then open a listing. See [How to find a listing](/buying/find-a-listing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the listing">
    Check the price, delivery method, seller delivery estimate, available stock, and the seller's description and delivery instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your quantity">
    Enter the quantity you want. Each listing shows its minimum order quantity and how many units are currently in stock. The total updates automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Buy now">
    Review your order summary and continue to checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Enter your payment details and confirm. You will receive confirmation once your payment has been processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After your payment is confirmed, the seller is notified and begins delivery. See [What happens after you pay](/buying/after-you-pay) for details.

<Note>
Make sure you have read the seller's delivery instructions before you buy. Some deliveries require information from you, such as an in-game username.
</Note>
