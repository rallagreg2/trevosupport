---
title: "Understanding price, quantity, and currency"
description: "Understand price per unit, minimum quantity, stock, and totals."
---

Many Trevo listings, such as in-game currency, are sold per unit. This article explains how the numbers on an offer page fit together.

## Price per unit

The price per unit is the cost of a single unit of the product. Because unit prices can be very small, they are shown with several decimal places.

## Minimum quantity and stock

Each listing sets a **minimum quantity**, which is the smallest amount you can order. The listing also shows the **stock**, which is the number of units the seller currently has available. You can order any quantity between the minimum and the available stock.

## Your total

Your total is calculated as:

**price per unit × quantity**

The total updates as you change the quantity, and is shown before you confirm your purchase.

## Currency

Prices are displayed in the currency selected in the language and currency menu in the footer of the site. If you change it, prices across Trevo update to match.

## Bundles and denominations

Some listings, particularly top ups, are sold in fixed bundles or denominations rather than by free quantity. In that case, select the bundle you want and the price for that bundle is shown.
