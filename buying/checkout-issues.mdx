---
title: "I can't complete checkout"
description: "Troubleshooting steps if your payment is declined or checkout fails."
---

If you are unable to complete a purchase, work through the checks below.

## Your payment was declined

- Confirm that your card number, expiry date, and security code are correct.
- Check that your card has sufficient funds and is enabled for online purchases.
- Contact your bank, as some banks block online payments for security reasons until you approve them.
- Try a different card or payment method.

## The page won't load or the button doesn't respond

- Refresh the page and try again.
- Make sure you are signed in to your Trevo account.
- Try a different browser, or disable extensions that may block payment pages.
- Check that your internet connection is stable.

## The quantity is not accepted

Each listing has a minimum order quantity and a limited stock. Make sure the quantity you entered falls between the two.

## You were charged but see no order

Check your email for an order confirmation. If you have been charged and cannot find your order, [contact Trevo Support](/contact-support) and include the time of the payment and the last four digits of your card.
