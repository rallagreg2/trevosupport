---
title: "What happens after you pay"
description: "How delivery works once your payment has been confirmed."
---

Once your payment is confirmed, your order moves into delivery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Payment is confirmed">
    You receive confirmation that your payment was successful, and the seller is notified of your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The seller delivers your order">
    The seller completes delivery using the delivery method shown on the listing, within the estimated delivery time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You receive your purchase">
    Follow any delivery instructions from the listing. Some deliveries require you to be online or to provide details such as a username.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delivery time

Every listing displays a **seller delivery estimate**. This is the time the seller expects to need to complete your order. Actual delivery is often faster, but may vary with the product and the seller's availability.

## Delivery methods

The delivery method (for example, a login method) is shown on the listing. Review the delivery instructions on the offer page before you purchase so you know what to expect.

## If something isn't right

If your order has not arrived within the estimated time, or you have a problem with it, see [I need help with an order](/faq/help-with-an-order).
