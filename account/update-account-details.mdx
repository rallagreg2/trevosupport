---
title: "Updating your account details"
description: "Change your password, email, and other account details."
---

You can review and update your account information from your account settings.

1. Sign in to Trevo.
2. Select your profile icon in the top right corner of the page.
3. Open your account settings.
4. Update your details and save your changes.

## Changing your password

In your account settings, choose the option to change your password. If you've forgotten your password, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page to reset it by email.

## Changing your email address

Your email address is used for order updates and account recovery. If you need to change it and can't do so in your settings, [contact Trevo Support](/contact-support).
