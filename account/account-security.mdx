---
title: "Protecting your Trevo account"
description: "Practical steps for keeping your Trevo account safe."
---

Your Trevo account protects your orders and your personal information. Follow these practices to keep it secure.

## Best practices

- **Use a strong, unique password** that you don't use on any other website.
- **Keep your email account secure**, since it can be used to reset your Trevo password.
- **Never share your password** or verification codes with anyone. Trevo staff will never ask for them.
- **Stay on Trevo.** Complete all purchases and sales through the marketplace so you are protected by our rules and policies.
- **Be cautious of links.** Only sign in through the official Trevo website.
- **Sign out** of shared or public devices.

## Recognizing scams

Be careful if someone:

- Asks you to pay or deliver outside of Trevo
- Pressures you to act quickly
- Sends you a link to a login page you don't recognize
- Claims to be Trevo staff and asks for your password

If you see suspicious behavior, [report a concern](/policies/report-a-concern).
