---
title: "Creating an account and verifying your email"
description: "Register a Trevo account and verify your email address."
---

A Trevo account lets you buy, sell, and manage your orders.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Select the sign-up option from the Trevo home page and enter your details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    We send a verification message to the email address you registered with. Open it and follow the link to confirm your address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using Trevo">
    Once your email is verified, you can browse listings, place orders, and apply to become a seller.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## I didn't receive the verification email

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Make sure you entered your email address correctly.
- Wait a few minutes, then request a new verification email from your account.

If you still can't verify your account, [contact Trevo Support](/contact-support).
