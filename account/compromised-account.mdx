---
title: "My account may have been accessed by someone else"
description: "Steps to take if you think someone else has accessed your account."
---

If you believe someone else has accessed your account, act quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Sign in if you still can, and change your password immediately. If you can't sign in, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure your email account">
    If your email account may also be affected, change that password too, and review any forwarding rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your activity">
    Check your recent orders and listings for anything you don't recognize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Trevo Support">
    [Contact us](/contact-support) as soon as possible and describe what happened. We will help you secure your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If you used the same password on other websites, change it there as well.
</Warning>
